Athens, Greece

"The Cradle of Western Civilization"

Annual Events & Festivals

  1. Athens & Epidaurus Festival (June - August)
    The city's most famous cultural event featuring theater, ballet, and jazz in ancient venues.

  2. Athens Authentic Marathon (November)
    Run the original route from the city of Marathon to Athens Panathenaic Stadium.

  3. August Full Moon Festival
    Archaeological sites open for free at night with live music under the moonlight.
