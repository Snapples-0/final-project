Athens, Greece

"The Cradle of Western Civilization"

Must-See Landmarks

1. The Parthenon

The symbol of Western Civilization. Built between 447 and 438 BC.

Fun Fact: The columns lean slightly inward to create an optical illusion of straightness.

2. The Ancient Agora

The heart of ancient Athens, the focus of political, commercial, administrative and social activity, the religious and cultural center, and the seat of justice.
