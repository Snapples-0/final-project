Athens, Greece

"The Cradle of Western Civilization"

Cultural Highlights

The Acropolis Museum
Consistently voted one of the best museums in the world. It houses every artifact found on the rock and on the surrounding slopes.
National Archaeological Museum
The largest museum in Greece and one of the world's great museums, containing the richest collection of artifacts from Greek antiquity worldwide.
Stavros Niarchos Foundation Cultural Center
A modern architectural masterpiece housing the National Library and the National Opera.
