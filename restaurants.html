A Taste of Athens

Greek cuisine is famous worldwide. Here are our top 3 picks.

1. Kuzina

Located in Thissio with a view of the Temple of Hephaestus. Modern Greek cuisine.

2. O Thanasis

Famous for the best kebab and souvlaki in the Monastiraki district.

3. Dionysos Zonar's

Fine dining right at the foot of the Acropolis. Unbeatable views.
