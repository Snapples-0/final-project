Frequently Asked Questions

What currency is used in Athens?
Greece is part of the EU and uses the Euro (€).
Is the tap water safe to drink?
Yes, the tap water in Athens is safe and high quality.
What is the best time to visit?
Spring (April-June) and Autumn (September-October) offer the best weather.
Do I need a visa?
US citizens can stay up to 90 days without a visa.
How do I get around?
The Athens Metro is clean, safe, and connects to the airport.
